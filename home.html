ANUV JAIN

Anuv Jain began his musical journey as an independent artist, creating and sharing his songs online without the backing of major music labels. His emotional lyrics and soothing melodies helped him build a deep connection with listeners across India. Songs like Baarishein, Gul, and Alag Aasmaan became massive hits among young audiences and established him as one of the leading indie artists in the country. Over time, he expanded his career through concerts and live performances, creating unforgettable experiences for fans.
